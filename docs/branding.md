# International Squirrel Machines — Branding

**Full name:** International Squirrel Machines  
**Short:** ISM  
**Motto:** Pure hardware for Squirrel-OS/2.

Visual language:
- Industrial, clean lines reminiscent of classic systems without copying any specific product.
- Squirrel motifs used sparingly on enclosures (badge, vent pattern, or end-cap).
- Color direction: warm grays, soft browns, and a single accent (to be finalized per device).

All branding assets (logos, badges, silkscreen legends) live in `common/branding/`.
