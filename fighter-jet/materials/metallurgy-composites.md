# Metallurgy & Composites Notes — ISM-FJ-1

Public alloy *families* used as reference only; ISM designations are internal and not claimed equivalent to any OEM spec.

## Alloy / composite families

### ISM-Al-7 (aluminum family)
- Role: longerons, bulkheads, spar caps (hybrid baseline).
- Traits: high strength, established repair tooling.
- Limits: fatigue at fastener holes — use cold working / interference fit where called.

### ISM-Ti-6 (titanium family)
- Role: gear, fittings, hot-adjacent structure.
- Traits: strength, corrosion, galvanic friendliness with CFRP vs bare Al.
- Limits: cost, machining time — reserve for high-load parts.

### ISM-CFRP-A (carbon/epoxy)
- Role: skins, ribs, control surfaces, fin.
- Layups: quasi-isotropic skins; tailored spar webs.
- Limits: impact damage inspectability; lightning strike mesh or foils required.

### ISM-RF-Q (radome composite)
- RF-transparent; rain erosion coating on leading surfaces.

### High-temp / nickel (engine bay stub)
- Firewall and nozzle-adjacent panels; details track propulsion vendor ICD.

## Fasteners
| Joint | Fastener intent |
|-------|-----------------|
| CFRP–CFRP | Ti fasteners, large footprint washers / inserts |
| CFRP–Al | Ti or CRES + sealant; no bare Al washers against carbon |
| Ti–Ti | Ti fasteners |
| Access panels | Captive fasteners; few tools for line maintenance |

## Thermal
- Avionics bay: ECS air + optional cold plate; CFRP trays OK below resin Tg margin.
- Aft fuselage: Ti + insulation; no unprotected epoxy in nozzle vicinity.
- Leading edges: erosion/thermal coating TBD with mission profile.

## Corrosion
- Filiform risk on Al under paint — chromate-free primer path preferred for ISM branding/environment goals.
- Dissimilar metal joints sealed; drain paths at WS/FS low points.
- Landing gear: Ti baseline reduces corrosion maintenance vs steel (steel still option for cost).
